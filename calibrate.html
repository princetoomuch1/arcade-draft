2 · Font sharpness (measured)

Desktop only, so the one density that must pass is CHUNKY; check FINE too if you use a 1440p or 4K monitor. Each cell renders real glyphs at that device-pixel size and counts pixels that are neither pure background nor pure foreground. Those are anti-aliased fringe. 0% = the glyph grid lands on whole device pixels. Lower is sharper. Threshold: under 2% passes, 2–8% is soft, over 8% is blurry.

3 · U grid

Heavy line every 8u, label every 32u. The checkerboard below it is 1u squares: if any square looks grey or uneven, u is landing on a fraction of a device pixel.
